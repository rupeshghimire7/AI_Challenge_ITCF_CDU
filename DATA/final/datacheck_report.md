Two rows are duplicates when they have the same text and the same `aspect_category` but come from different sentences (or reviews). Rows without an opinion count too (empty category = same category). Two rows of one sentence with opposite polarities are not duplicates; they are flagged `polarity_conflict`. Every duplicate row is flagged in the `duplicate_in` column of the output CSVs; nothing is removed.
